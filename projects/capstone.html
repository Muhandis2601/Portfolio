CAPSTONE – Robot Line Follower dengan Kamera Pixy 2

Self-Driving Line Follower Robot with Pixy 2 Vision System

Sekolah Vokasi Universitas Gadjah Mada

Period February 2024 – June 2024
Institution Sekolah Vokasi UGM
Category Robotics & Computer Vision
Status Completed

Project Overview

About the Project

The CAPSTONE project is the final integrative project of the vocational program, combining all engineering disciplines learned throughout the course. This project involved designing and building a self-driving line follower robot enhanced with a Pixy 2 vision camera for advanced color-based object detection and tracking.

The robot's mechanical structure was fully designed using Autodesk Inventor, including the chassis, wheel assemblies, motor mounts, and camera mounting bracket. The Pixy 2 camera system enables the robot to detect colored lines and objects, allowing it to follow a predetermined path intelligently while also recognizing specific color signatures.

The integration of DC gearbox motors, ultrasonic sensors, and the Pixy 2 camera creates a multi-sensor autonomous navigation system. The complete 3D assembly model was developed in Autodesk Inventor to ensure proper component fitment and mechanical integrity before physical fabrication.
