Project Overview

About the Project

The Gambar Teknik (Technical Drawing) course project encompassed a series of 3D modeling and technical drawing assignments using SketchUp software. The course focused on developing spatial thinking, technical illustration, and 3D visualization skills — essential competencies for engineering design and product development.

The primary project deliverables included a virtual keyboard design, a complete house architectural model, and a furniture piece (lemari/wardrobe). Each design required precise dimensioning, accurate proportioning, and multi-view technical drawings following standard engineering conventions.

Throughout the course, proficiency was built in SketchUp's modeling tools, layer management, material rendering, and generating orthographic projections. The projects also involved creating product description documents and presentation materials to communicate design intent professionally.
