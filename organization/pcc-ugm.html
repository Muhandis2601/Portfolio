Reasoning Center
Community UGM

General Chairman · May 2024 – May 2025

Penalaran Center Community (PCC) is a student reasoning and scientific writing community under Sekolah Vokasi Universitas Gadjah Mada, focused on developing students' critical thinking, essay writing, and scientific paper competition skills. As General Chairman, I led the organization's overall direction, coordinated the management board, and represented PCC in university-level forums.
