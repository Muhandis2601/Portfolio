Gadjah Mada Event
in Bekasi (GMEB)

Sponsorship Committee · 8th GMEB 2023

Gadjah Mada Event in Bekasi (GMEB) is an annual event organized by UGM alumni and student communities to strengthen connections between Gadjah Mada University and the Bekasi region. As a member of the Sponsorship Committee for the 8th edition, I contributed to securing funding partnerships and coordinating sponsor relations to support the event's execution.
